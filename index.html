Reproduction

Asexual: reproduces solo, child is a mutated clone. Sexual: two ready adults of this species have to physically meet — their offspring's traits and brain are shuffled from both parents, then mutated. Harder to pull off, more genetic mixing.

Sexual reproducers in particular need a decent founding population — a mate has to actually be nearby and ready too, so a handful of individuals can struggle against an established rival for the same food.
