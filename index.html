Free amino acids and nucleotides drift in a dish, occasionally bonding into peptides and RNA when energy happens to be nearby. Folded peptides can become catalysts; RNA long and structured enough can start templating copies of itself, mutations and all. Lipids self-assemble into membranes for free — no energy or catalyst needed — and a membrane that closes around a working replicator becomes a protocell. Nothing here is scripted to succeed.

The dish starts empty — populate it from Origins (evolved from chemistry) or design a body plan by hand here and release it. Its brain starts random — evolution shapes how it actually behaves over the generations that follow.

Reproduction

Asexual: reproduces solo, child is a mutated clone. Sexual: two ready adults of this species have to physically meet — their offspring's traits and brain are shuffled from both parents, then mutated. Harder to pull off, more genetic mixing.

Organelles 0 / 10

Every organelle has a running energy cost, so a loadout is a bet, not a free upgrade — evolution will prune what doesn't pay for itself. A virtunism with zero mouths and zero chloroplasts can't feed itself at all.

Sexual reproducers in particular need a decent founding population — a mate has to actually be nearby and ready too, so a handful of individuals can struggle against an established rival for the same food.
